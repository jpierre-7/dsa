# Data Structures & Algorithms Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Problem Solving with Algorithms and Data Structures using Python_, 3rd ed. — Miller & Ranum (Runestone, free)](https://runestone.academy/ns/books/published/pythonds3/index.html)
  Free interactive textbook written in Python. Use for: the main reading for each topic, especially [Big-O](https://runestone.academy/ns/books/published/pythonds3/AlgorithmAnalysis/BigONotation.html) and [performance of Python data structures](https://runestone.academy/ns/books/published/pythonds3/AlgorithmAnalysis/PerformanceofPythonDataStructures.html).
- [Course: MIT 6.006 _Introduction to Algorithms_, Spring 2020 (OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-spring-2020/)
  Demaine, Ku & Solomon. Lecture videos and notes. Use for: rigorous depth when the Runestone treatment feels thin, e.g. hashing, sorting lower bounds, graph algorithms, DP. [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP63EdVPNLG3ToM6LaEUuStEY).
- [Reference: Python Wiki, _TimeComplexity_](https://wiki.python.org/moin/TimeComplexity)
  Canonical cost table for CPython list, deque, set, and dict operations. Use for: any claim about what a built-in operation costs.
- [Guide: _Tech Interview Handbook_, Yangshun Tay](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/study-cheatsheet/)
  Free, by the author of Blind 75. Use for: interview-specific cheat sheets, corner cases, curated practice lists, and interview conduct.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Python Discord](https://www.pythondiscord.com/)
  Large, well-moderated server with volunteer helpers. Use for: getting a second pair of eyes on a solution or a complexity argument.
- [LeetCode Discuss](https://leetcode.com/discuss/)
  Every problem has a discussion and solutions tab. Use for: comparing your solution to others after you've submitted your own attempt.

## Gaps
- A good source for practising talking through a solution out loud (mock interviews). Look at peer mock-interview platforms once core patterns are in place.
